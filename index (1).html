Line Pals: Arrow Escape Puzzle

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026 · App ID: com.softdev.linepals

In short: Line Pals has no accounts and we do not ask for your name, email or contacts. Your game progress stays on your phone. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which collects some device data to show and measure ads. Purchases are handled by Google Play.

Who we are

Line Pals ("the app") is developed by HamzaLabs ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information is collected when you use the app and how it is used.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, photos, contacts, messages or precise location. The app has no sign-in and no chat.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (levels, stars, coins, hints, settings and purchase status) is saved only on your device. We do not receive it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads, including optional rewarded video ads, using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). To show, measure and protect ads, Google may automatically collect and process:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID
  • IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location
  • App interaction data, such as app launches, taps and video views
  • Diagnostic data, such as app and ad performance

This data is encrypted in transit. Google uses it as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's Android settings. Buying "Remove Ads" turns off ad breaks in the app.

In-app purchases (Google Play)

Hint packs and "Remove Ads" are sold through Google Play Billing. Google processes your payment; we never see your card or payment details. The app only receives confirmation of which items you bought so it can deliver them. See the Google Privacy Policy and Google Play Terms of Service.

How information is used

  • To run the game and remember your progress on your device
  • To show ads and give rewards for rewarded ads
  • To deliver and restore in-app purchases
  • To prevent fraud and keep the app working well

We do not sell your personal information.

Children

Line Pals is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

Your choices and rights

  • Turn off personalized ads or reset your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Delete all game data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access or delete data. Because we do not hold personal data, requests about ad data should go to Google; we are happy to help you with that.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The effective date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be noted in the app's store listing.
